What it shows

Everything you'd glance at your phone for.

The island stays a small black pill until something happens. Point at it to peek, click to open, right-click for the menu. It never steals focus from what you're doing.

Music

Album art and live audio bars.

Spotify works out of the box, no account or API key. The bars move with the actual sound and take their color from the album art.

  • Seek, skip and pause from the island
  • Play/pause and next when you point at the pill
  • Browsers, Apple Music and other players too, if you want

Real screenshots from the app

Claude Code

Know when Claude is done, without watching the terminal.

A spinning Claude logo and a timer while it works. "Claude is done" with the project and how long it took, and "Claude needs you" when it's waiting for you.

  • Every running session, with its project and time
  • Set up with one script, your other settings are kept
  • Two things at once? The second one waits in the small circle

Click the empty island to start one

Timers & focus

A timer, a Pomodoro and a stopwatch, one click away.

Click the empty island, pick a preset or scroll the minutes, and the countdown lives in the pill. Focus sessions run work and breaks back to back.

  • An alarm that keeps ringing until you stop it
  • Focus, break and long-break lengths you set yourself
  • Recent: the last ten alerts, also the ones you missed

Messages & calls

Messages pop out of the island. Calls get a call card.

Pick the chat apps you care about. Their messages pop out of the island in their brand color; click one to jump straight to the conversation.

  • Answer or decline calls from Discord, WhatsApp, Teams and more
  • Follows Windows' Do Not Disturb
  • Quiet times per add-on, like no clipboard pop-ups at night

For developers

Your scripts get an island too.

A tiny local API: a backup, a build or a long download can show its own pop-ups, progress and buttons in the island. Only programs on your PC can use it, web pages can't.

PowerShell
PS> island.ps1 -Progress build -Title "Building" -Value 0.6
PS> island.ps1 -Title "Build failed" -Icon error -Color red `
      -Button "log=Open log" -Button "retry=Retry" -Wait
retry
PS> curl -X POST http://127.0.0.1:47823/alert `
      -d '{"title":"Backup done","icon":"success"}'
{"ok":true,"id":"4f9c2e1a07"}
PS>
Build failed
3 errors in App.xaml.cs
Open log Retry
60%

POST /alert · POST /progress · GET /alert/{id}

Open source

If you don't like something, change it.

  • Add an activity. A new thing in the pill is one small class.
  • Add a language. A test tells you exactly which texts are still missing.
  • Build your own. One command publishes the island, the settings app and the installer.
